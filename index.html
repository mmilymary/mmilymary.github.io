I'm Mila – a mother of four, an artist and an IT business student at TAMK in Tampere.

I graduated as a cosmetologist in December 2024, but I never ended up working in the field. Even during those studies it was clear where my curiosity kept pulling me: technology, digital art, advertising and business. In my free time I make mixed media art – watercolours, coloured pencils, acrylics, ink markers and whatever else the piece asks for.

While at home with my youngest, I decided to apply to IT, and the field surprised me with how wide it is. It brings together everything I enjoy, and much of it can be done on my own terms.

I'm most drawn to UX/UI design, where I can bring my own creativity and hand-drawn touch into interfaces. I'm also drawn to AI studies and to the business side of my BBA degree. Step by step I'm diving into code by building my own projects and sketching concepts in my free time.
